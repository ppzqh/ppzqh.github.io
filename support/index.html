FAQ

Common questions

How do I report a bug?

Email the support address above or open an issue on GitHub. Include reproduction steps and screenshots when possible.

How do I request account or data deletion?

Send an email from the account you use in Pet Diary and describe the deletion request. We will process the request according to the privacy policy.
