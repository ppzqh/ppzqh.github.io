FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I start a game with nearby friends?

One player taps “创建牌桌 (Create Table)” to host the room, while other players open the app on their iPhones. As long as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are turned on (or devices are on the same Wi-Fi router), the host's room will automatically appear under “附近可加入牌桌 (Nearby Tables)”. Tap to request a seat and enter the table!

2. Does AirPoker require internet access or mobile data?

No internet is required. AirPoker communicates directly between local devices using Apple's Bonjour and peer-to-peer wireless technologies. You can play smoothly during outdoor camping, flights, or places with poor cellular reception.

3. Are the chips real money?

No. AirPoker is purely an offline dealing and scorekeeping tool for friendly game nights. All chips and balances are virtual counters and cannot be exchanged for cash, real goods, or virtual currency.

4. Can I play or test alone without nearby players?

Yes! AirPoker includes a built-in “电脑陪练模式 (Practice with Bots)”. When creating a table, open the rule settings drawer and enable the “电脑陪练模式” toggle. Once in the game, the host can tap “+ 电脑” to add automated computer opponents and test or practice full poker hands.

5. What if nearby devices cannot find each other?

  • Ensure both devices have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth switched on in iOS Settings.
  • Make sure you have granted the “Local Network (本地网络)” permission when prompted by iOS. You can verify this in Settings > AirPoker > Local Network.
  • If on a corporate or public guest Wi-Fi network with AP client isolation enabled, simply turn off Wi-Fi on both devices to let Apple peer-to-peer direct connect take over.

6. Where can I read the Privacy Policy?

The AirPoker Privacy Policy is available at /airpoker/privacy/.
