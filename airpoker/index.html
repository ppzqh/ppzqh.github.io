iOS App

AirPoker

Face-to-face Texas Hold'em poker dealer and chip manager. Play with friends nearby over Apple Local Network (Bonjour) with zero internet and zero cellular data required.

Support & FAQ Privacy Policy

Key Highlights

Zero Data

100% Offline Play

Connect instantly with nearby iPhones using Apple Bonjour and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi/Bluetooth. No internet connection, no servers, and no cellular data consumed.

Table Experience

Authentic Felt Layout

Faithfully crafted after modern mobile poker tables. Features live pot calculation, side pots, smooth card animations, all-in board runouts, and quick raise adjustment sliders.

Bookkeeping

Live Ledger & PnL

Automatically tracks buy-ins, rebuys, and net profit/loss for every seated player. No need for manual pen-and-paper math after the game.

Solo & Review

Practice with Bots

Want to practice strategy or test game mechanics on your own? Toggle the built-in Practice Mode in room settings to seat intelligent computer opponents.

Compliance

Fair Play & Disclaimer

AirPoker is designed strictly for social, in-person entertainment and scorekeeping among friends. It contains no real-money gambling, cash payouts, or in-app token purchases.
