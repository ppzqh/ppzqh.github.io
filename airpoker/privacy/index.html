Privacy Policy

AirPoker is built with privacy as a foundational principle. It is designed as a 100% offline, local face-to-face game tool that does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

AirPoker is developed and provided by Qiheng Zhou. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information collection

No Personal Data Collected

AirPoker does not require user accounts, logins, or cloud registration. We do not collect, track, or harvest your personal information (such as name, phone number, email address, contacts, or biometric data).

  • Player Nickname: The nickname you set in the app is saved exclusively on your local device (via local storage) and is broadcast strictly to nearby peers on the local table. It is never transmitted to any external server.
  • Game Data: All hand records, chip stacks, and ledger entries exist solely in memory and local session state during an active game.

2. Device & network permissions

Local Network & Bluetooth

AirPoker utilizes local device capabilities to enable offline multiplayer gameplay without requiring internet access:

  • Local Network (Bonjour): Used to discover and establish direct peer-to-peer connections with friends in the same physical space. All communications travel point-to-point over your local Wi-Fi or ad-hoc wireless connection.
  • Bluetooth: Used to assist with initial device discovery and local handshakes when no Wi-Fi access point is present.
  • No Internet Routing: Game state and peer traffic never leave your local environment and are never routed through or stored on external cloud servers.

3. Virtual chips & disclaimer

No Real-Money Gambling

AirPoker is strictly a scorekeeping and dealing utility for friendly, in-person entertainment:

  • All chips, bets, and pot values in the app are purely virtual counters for scorekeeping.
  • The app does not support real-money transactions, in-app purchases of virtual currency, deposits, or cash withdrawals.
  • The virtual chips have no cash or monetary value.

4. Third-party services

Zero Ads & Trackers

AirPoker contains no advertising frameworks, no third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase), and no cross-site tracking technologies. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Children's Privacy Protection

Because AirPoker collects no personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.
